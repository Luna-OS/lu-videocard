# Lu Videocard 1.1.0

The GPU enhancement update: Lu Videocard now does to the capture card's picture what Veyra does,
on every GPU, in the Moon look.

- **Enhance** (E): a GPU pipeline on every frame – AMD FSR 1.0 upscaling (EASU) to the window's
  size, RCAS sharpening, spatial and temporal denoise, FXAA anti-aliasing, an HDR look (local
  contrast, deeper shadows, brighter lights), colour controls (brightness, contrast, saturation,
  vibrance, gamma, temperature, tint) and .cube LUTs. Presets: Clean, Sharp, Cinema, Retro, Max.
- **Frame generation** (G): a generated frame between every two real ones, from symmetric
  block-matching motion estimation with protection against artefacts; doubles 60 fps to 120. It
  drops the frames a capture card repeats (a 30 fps game becomes a smooth 60) and pauses by
  itself on a screen that can't show twice the rate.
- **Recording** (F8): the picture as shown, with the card's sound, as MP4 (H.264) or WebM at a
  chosen bitrate, into Videos › Lu Videocard. **Replay buffer** (F9): the last 15 s to 2 min,
  saved on a key press.
- **Performance info** (I): frames per second, 1 % low, frame time, GPU time, generated frames
  and dropped repeats, next to the picture and sound info.
- Screenshots (F7) now capture the enhanced picture at its output resolution.
- Without a usable GPU the picture is shown as before.
